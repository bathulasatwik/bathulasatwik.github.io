My research focuses on understanding "learning" in both artificial as well as biological systems through the study of neural representations. I am interested in how representations emerge, evolve through learning, encode information, and support generalization, memorization, adaptation and intelligent behaviour.

I seek to understand the fundamental principles that govern "learning" across artificial and biological intelligence.
